Segundo ele, o Jason entra no tráfico e vira assaltante de banco por causa das circunstâncias da vida dele. Mas cabe ao jogador decidir se ele precisa mesmo disso, ou se é uma escolha de quem "é diferente das outras pessoas".

🎯 Rockstar just opened up about Jason like never before.

Rupert Humphries, Rockstar's senior vice president of narrative, told LOVE Magazine the team "didn't want to make any kind of statement with Jason's backstory."

He says Jason turns to drug trafficking and bank robbery "because of his material circumstances." But "a lot of this is up to the player to decide whether he needs to do that, or if it's a choice he makes because he's wired differently from other people."

And Lucia "had to be someone who wanted a better life for herself" and wouldn't let any obstacle stop her. The two were built to complement each other.

GTA VI drops November 19.
